Tell a parent and call the bank or card company right away — most apps also let you lock the card. Fast reporting limits what you can lose: for credit cards it's capped at $50, but for debit cards the limit grows the longer you wait. Check your statement for charges you didn't make, and report fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
