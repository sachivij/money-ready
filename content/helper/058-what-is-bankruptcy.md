question: What is bankruptcy?
tags: bankruptcy, bankrupt
source: courts
approved_by: Sumeet

---
Bankruptcy is a legal process, handled by federal courts, for people or businesses that can't pay their debts. It can wipe out or reorganize some debts, but it stays on a credit report for years and makes borrowing harder. Student loans usually can't be erased this way.
