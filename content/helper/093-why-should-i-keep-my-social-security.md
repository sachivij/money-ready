---
question: Why should I keep my Social Security number private?
tags: social security number, ssn, social security card
source: ssa
approved_by: Sumeet
approved_on: 2026-10-30
---
Your Social Security number is a key that unlocks credit and other accounts in your name. Don't carry your card, and only give the number when it's truly required, like for a job or a bank. Ask a parent before sharing it anywhere.
