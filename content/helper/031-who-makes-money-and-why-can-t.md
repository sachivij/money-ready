Paper bills are printed by the U.S. Bureau of Engraving and Printing and coins are made by the U.S. Mint. The Federal Reserve decides how much money goes into the economy. Printing lots of extra money doesn't make people richer — it makes prices go up, because more money is chasing the same amount of goods.
