Under federal law, most jobs require you to be at least 14, and 14- and 15-year-olds have limits on hours and on the kinds of work they can do. Younger kids can do some jobs, like babysitting, delivering newspapers, or working in a family business. Virginia requires a work permit for 14- and 15-year-olds.
