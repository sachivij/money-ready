question: What is a payday loan?

approved_by: Sumeet

A payday loan is a small, short-term loan meant to be repaid on your next payday. They often have very high costs — fees that can equal an APR of 300% or more — and can trap borrowers in a cycle of re-borrowing. An emergency fund is a safer backup.
