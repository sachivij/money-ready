question: How do I keep my accounts and passwords safe?
tags: password, strong password, two-factor, 2fa, two step, hacked, online safety, internet safety, account security, verification code

approved_by: Sumeet

Use a long, different password for each account, turn on two-step verification, and never share codes sent to your phone. Don't click links in unexpected messages. If an account is hacked, change the password right away and tell a trusted adult.
