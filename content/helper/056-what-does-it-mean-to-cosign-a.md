---
question: What does it mean to cosign a loan?
tags: cosign, co-sign, cosigner, co-signer
source: ftc
approved_by: Sumeet
approved_on: 2026-10-30
---
A cosigner promises to pay a loan if the borrower doesn't. It's a big responsibility: missed payments hurt the cosigner's credit too, and the lender can ask the cosigner for the full amount.
