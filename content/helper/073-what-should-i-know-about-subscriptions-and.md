question: What should I know about subscriptions and free trials?
tags: subscription, free trial, auto-renew, automatic renewal, cancel a subscription, monthly charge

approved_by: Sumeet

Many free trials turn into paid subscriptions automatically unless you cancel in time. Before signing up, check the price after the trial and how to cancel, and set a reminder. Small monthly charges add up over a year.
