Insurance is a plan where you pay a small, regular amount (a premium) so that a big, unexpected cost gets covered. Common types are health insurance (doctor and hospital bills), car insurance (accidents, and required by law to drive), homeowners or renters insurance (your home and belongings), and life insurance (helps a family if someone dies). A deductible is the amount you pay yourself before the insurance starts paying.
