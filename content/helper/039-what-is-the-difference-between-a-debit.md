---
question: What is the difference between a debit card and a credit card?
tags: debit, debit card, credit card, debit or credit, debit vs credit
source: cfpb
approved_by: Sumeet
approved_on: 2026-10-30
---
A debit card spends money that is already in your bank account. A credit card borrows money you have to pay back later, and if you don't pay the full bill each month you are charged interest.
