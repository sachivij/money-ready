question: What are CDs and money market accounts?

source: fdic
approved_by: Sumeet

A certificate of deposit (CD) pays a set interest rate if you leave money in it for a set time, like a year; taking it out early usually costs a penalty. Money market and high-yield savings accounts often pay more interest than regular savings. At an FDIC-insured bank, all of these are insured.
