---
question: How do I recognize an investment scam?
tags: get rich quick, guaranteed return, ponzi, pyramid scheme, mlm, too good to be true, investment scam
source: sec
approved_by: Sumeet
approved_on: 2026-10-30

Watch for promises of guaranteed high returns, pressure to act fast, and pitches to recruit friends. Ponzi and pyramid schemes pay early investors with money from newer ones until they collapse. If it sounds too good to be true, it probably is.
