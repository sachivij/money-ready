question: What is the difference between salary and hourly pay?
tags: salary, hourly, hourly wage, overtime, paid per hour
source: dol
approved_by: Sumeet

---
Hourly workers are paid for each hour they work, and most get overtime — 1.5 times their rate — for hours over 40 in a week. Salaried workers earn a set amount per year no matter the exact hours, and many don't get overtime.
