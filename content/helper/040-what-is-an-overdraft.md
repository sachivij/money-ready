---
question: What is an overdraft?

approved_by: Sumeet
approved_on: 2026-10-30
---
An overdraft happens when you spend more than you have in your account. The bank may cover it but charge a fee, or decline the purchase. Checking your balance before you spend and turning on low-balance alerts help you avoid overdraft fees.
