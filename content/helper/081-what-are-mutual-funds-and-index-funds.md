question: What are mutual funds and index funds?

A mutual fund pools money from many investors to buy lots of stocks or bonds at once. An index fund is a kind of fund that simply follows a market index, like the S&P 500, usually with low fees. Owning many investments at once — diversification — lowers the risk of one bad pick.
