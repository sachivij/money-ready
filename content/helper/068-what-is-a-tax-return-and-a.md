---
question: What is a tax return and a tax refund?
tags: tax return, tax refund, file taxes, filing taxes, file my taxes, do my taxes
source: irs
approved_by: Sumeet
approved_on: 2026-10-30
---
Once a year, people file a tax return to report what they earned and check whether the right amount of tax was taken from their pay. If too much was withheld, they get the difference back as a refund; if too little, they owe more. Teens with a job may get a refund by filing even when they don't have to.
