Start with a story or a game, then connect it to one big idea, like needs vs. wants or saving for a goal. Keep it about choices, not amounts, and never ask students about family income. The Teen Teach-In lessons in this toolkit's Modules and Workshop Builder give a ready-made plan for Grades 1–4.
