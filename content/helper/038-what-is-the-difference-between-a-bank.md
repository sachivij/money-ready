---
question: What is the difference between a bank and a credit union?
tags: credit union, ncua, bank vs credit union, bank or credit union
source: ncua
approved_by: Sumeet
approved_on: 2026-10-30
---
Both keep your money and make loans. A bank is a business owned by investors; a credit union is a not-for-profit owned by its members, so it may offer lower fees or better rates. Credit union deposits are insured by the NCUA, just like the FDIC insures banks.
