question: What is the economy and what is a recession?
tags: economy, recession, depression, gdp, economic
source: fed
approved_by: Sumeet
approved_on: 2026-10-30
---
The economy is all the making, buying, and selling of goods and services in a place. A recession is when the economy shrinks for a while — businesses sell less and some people lose jobs. Recessions eventually end, which is one reason an emergency fund matters.
