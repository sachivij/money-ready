question: What are the forms you fill out for a job, like a W-4 or W-2?

A W-4 is the form you fill out when you start a job so your employer knows how much tax to hold back from each paycheck. A W-2 is the form your employer sends after the year ends showing what you earned and the taxes withheld — you use it to file your tax return.
